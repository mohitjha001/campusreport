FAQ

Questions students may have

What if my university is not listed?

Click “Add missing university”, enter its name, city, state and type, then continue directly to the problem form. The university can remain pending until a moderator verifies it.

Can I post anonymously?

Yes. The problem form includes an anonymous posting option. Your public post can show “Anonymous” instead of your name.

Will every university be verified?

The production directory can be seeded from authoritative sources such as UGC and then moderated. User-submitted universities can carry a pending label until reviewed.

Is CampusReport only for complaints?

No. The goal is a community layer around universities: problems, discussions, voting, reviews, updates and eventually structured university information.
